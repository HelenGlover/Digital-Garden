Learning in Public Log

This project is an attempt to make something usually invisible in community organizing visible: feedback.

The best communities are built by the people who represent them. In many spaces, you give feedback but never see what happens next. Was it used? Was it valued? Did it actually change anything?

In helping run Civic Tech DC, it has become important to build a community that is also reflective of itself, one where you can see how feedback leads to new processes. I share the feedback I have received, my personal thoughts, and the next steps taken, if any.

This is an experiment I plan to update over the next few months. You are always welcome to share feedback.
